Jannik Lepper

PhD student
Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
Philipps-Universität Marburg, Germany

I am a PhD student at Philipps-Universität Marburg, working at the intersection of computational psychiatry, clinical neuroscience, and neuroimaging. My research examines neurobiological heterogeneity across affective and psychotic disorders and asks whether data driven subtypes and stages remain stable and clinically informative over time.

Research Interests

Computational psychiatry: Transdiagnostic stratification, psychiatric heterogeneity, Subtype and Stage Inference, machine learning.
Clinical neuroscience: Affective and psychotic disorders, longitudinal illness course, cognition, psychopathology, and psychosocial functioning.
Neuroimaging: Structural MRI, gray matter volume, and longitudinal neuroimaging.
Quantitative methods: Statistical modeling, longitudinal analysis, cross validation, and reproducible research workflows.
