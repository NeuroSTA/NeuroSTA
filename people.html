Interested in Joining the Group?

We are happy to hear from motivated students who would like to work with us.

We regularly offer:

  • Bachelor’s and Master’s theses (e.g., in psychology, neuroscience, cognitive science, linguistics, data science)
  • Medical doctoral projects in psychiatry / clinical neuroscience
  • Research internships / lab rotations for students interested in clinical and computational neuroscience

Possible topics include (but are not limited to):

  • Speech and language markers of affective and psychotic disorders
  • Transdiagnostic symptom dimensions and trajectories of mental illness
  • Multimodal neuroimaging (structural and functional MRI)
  • Computational psychiatry and machine learning approaches in mental health

If you are interested in working with us, please send an email to 📧 neurosta@uni-marburg.de.

We particularly welcome applications from students who enjoy interdisciplinary work at the interface of clinical research, neuroimaging, and language/speech analysis.
