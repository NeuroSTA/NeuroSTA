Rieke Mülfarth

Doctoral Researcher

Philipps-University Marburg, Germany
ORCID: 0009-0001-8425-0431

📧 r.r.muelfarth@uni-marburg.de

I’m a linguist, and my research focuses on latent linguistic parameters and their association with psychopathology, cognition, and brain structure in affective and psychotic disorders. By combining linguistic feature extraction using Natural Language Processing (NLP) with methods such as network analysis, diffusion-MRI-based connectomics, I am following an interdisciplinary approach that integrates linguistics, psychiatry, and computational neuroscience. Alongside my studies, I worked as a student (later research) assistant in the CRC 901 “On-The-Fly Computing” (2019-2022), where I annotated data. After that, I contributed to the CRC/TRR 318 “Constructing Explainability” (2022-2023), where my work included annotating and analyzing transcripts as well as conducting study sessions (incl. preparing the experimental setup/equipment & running participants). These experiences strengthend my deep interest in interdisciplinary collaboration and computational approaches to language. In addition, now I am a part of the Collaborative Research Center CRC/TRR 393 “Trajectories of Affective Disorders”.
